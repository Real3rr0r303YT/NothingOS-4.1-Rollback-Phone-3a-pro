## 🚀 How to Use (Installation Guide)

### Prerequisites:
1. **ADB & Fastboot Drivers** installed on your PC.
2. **Unlocked Bootloader** on your Nothing Phone (3a).
3. Battery charged above 50%.

### Steps:
1. **Boot into Fastboot Mode:**
   - Turn off your Nothing Phone (3a).
   - Press and hold **Volume Down + Power Button** together until you enter the Fastboot menu.
   - Connect your phone to your PC via a USB cable.

2. **Verify Fastboot Connection:**
   Open Command Prompt in your extracted folder and run:
   ```cmd
   fastboot devices
   ```

3. **Run the Flashing Script:**
   - Open the extracted folder.
   - Double-click **`flash_all.bat`** (or run `flash_all.bat` inside Command Prompt).

4. **Wait for Completion:**
   - Allow the script to automatically flash all partitions. Do not disconnect the cable!
   - Once finished, your device will automatically reboot into official Nothing OS 4.1 setup.
