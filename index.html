🚀 Installation & Extraction Guide

  1. Download All Parts: Go to the Release page above and download all split archive parts into the same folder on your device.
  2. Extract with ZArchiver / 7-Zip:
    • On Android (ZArchiver): Open ZArchiver, select the first part of the archive (e.g. .zip or .z01 / .001), tap it, and select "Extract here". The app automatically detects all parts and merges them.
    • On PC (7-Zip / WinRAR): Keep all parts in one folder, right-click the main file, and select 7-Zip -> Extract Here.
  3. Boot into Fastboot: Turn off your phone, then hold Volume Down + Power until the Fastboot screen appears.
  4. Connect & Flash: Plug your phone into your PC, open the extracted folder, and execute flash_all.bat. Your device will flash and reboot into Nothing OS 4.1.
